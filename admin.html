How to Use Inventory

Name a blank card, add its product types, and save. Product descriptions are optional. Use Ingredients: at the start when the text belongs in an Ingredients section. Images can be cropped before upload, and each card can display up to three.

Fresh and frozen quantities are separate. A frozen choice appears only when Offer Frozen Option is selected and frozen quantity is above zero.
