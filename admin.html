Inventory Hub

Edit images, prices, quantities, order limits, descriptions, and availability. Descriptions are optional; when entered, they appear on the product cards. Begin the text with Ingredients: to place it inside an Ingredients section. Completed new products move into their category automatically, and a fresh blank slot replaces each one.

Maximum per order: enter the most one household can request in a single order, or leave it blank for no limit. For a new product slot, enter the product details and save. It will move into that category's Current Products list and a new blank slot will appear. Check Available to order when it is ready for customers. Choose Made to order when the product does not use a fixed quantity.

Available to order: Use Save Changes when finished.
